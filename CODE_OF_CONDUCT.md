# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Tirotir project aims to provide a welcoming, respectful, and constructive
environment for everyone who participates in the project.

Participants are expected to communicate professionally and respectfully,
regardless of background, experience level, nationality, language, or technical
role.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of constructive behavior include:

* being respectful and considerate;
* welcoming questions from beginners;
* providing useful technical feedback;
* focusing criticism on ideas and implementations rather than individuals;
* accepting good-faith differences of opinion;
* respecting project maintainers, contributors, and users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* harassment or personal attacks;
* threats or intimidation;
* discriminatory or hateful conduct;
* deliberate disruption of project activities;
* publishing private information without permission;
* impersonation or deceptive representation of project maintainers;
* other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take reasonable action in response to violations,
including warnings, removal of inappropriate content, temporary restrictions,
or removal from project spaces.

Serious or repeated violations may result in permanent restrictions.

## Reporting

Concerns about conduct should be reported privately through an official
project communication channel.

Please provide enough information for the maintainers to understand the
situation and investigate it appropriately.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issue discussions,
pull requests, official project communication spaces, and other spaces where
participants are representing the Tirotir project.
